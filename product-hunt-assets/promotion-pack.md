## 3️⃣ Hacker News — Show HN

**标题：**
```
Show HN: Evolutionary Prophecy – Predict the future through evolutionary branches
```

**正文：**
```
What if we framed future predictions the way evolution frames species — as branching trees?

I built a small community platform where any "what happens next?" question becomes an evolutionary tree with branches. People vote on the branch they think is most likely, and the community sees where collective intelligence lands.

Examples already on the site:
- "Will cities become fully autonomous?" → 5 branches
- "What is the next step in human evolution?" → 5 branches
- "How will AI reshape work by 2030?" → 5 branches

No accounts — you just vote and move on. Categories: Technology, Biology, Culture, Life, Society.

Tech: Next.js 14 + SQLite + Prisma, running on a Mac Mini behind a Cloudflare Tunnel. Zero-cost infra; one goal was to see how far a solo dev + $0/month can go.

Honestly the hardest part isn't the code — it's the chicken-and-egg of a prediction community: predictions are only interesting when there's a crowd. Would love honest feedback on what would make you come back.

https://prophet.lifelong-growth.com
```
